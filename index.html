I'm Fengzhe Zhang. I'm currently a researcher in the school of computer science at Fudan University. My research focuses on artificial general intelligence (AGI) and embodied robotics driven by AGI brain. More specifically, I am interested in digging into the root of the learning mechanism in the biological world and developing models that reverse-engineer the human brain. My research direction is based on the idea that large language models (LLMs) haven't touched the core of intelligence yet. It calculates the probability based on the frequency that two entities appear together, which is just one of the many dimensions of information a system can learn from the raw input. Biological systems can learn much deeper information than LLMs. For example, a crow can learn to adapt to a new environment and solve problems it has never encountered before, with only a few tries. I believe an AGI system should be learning much more efficiently, flexibly, with much less power than that is used today. I am also passionate about collaborating with other researchers and contributing to the broader community through open-source projects.
